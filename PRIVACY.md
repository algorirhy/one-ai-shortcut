# Privacy / 隐私说明

## English

One Shortcut for AI Chat does not collect, sell, or retain personal information, prompts, browsing history, or usage analytics. It has no developer-operated backend.

When you submit a prompt, the extension processes it in memory and sends it only to the AI assistants selected in the popup. Those services receive and process the prompt according to their own terms and privacy policies. The extension does not intercept or store the assistants' responses.

The extension requests only these Chrome permissions:

- `tabs`: locate an existing supported assistant tab, navigate it to a new chat, or create a background tab.
- `notifications`: show a local system notification naming assistants whose delivery failed.

Website access is limited by the manifest to the six supported AI services. The extension does not use remote code, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

Questions or reports can be submitted through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/algorirhy/one-ai-shortcut/issues).

## 简体中文

One Shortcut for AI Chat 不会收集、出售或保留个人信息、提示词、浏览记录或使用分析数据，也没有由开发者运营的后端服务。

提交提示词时，扩展只在内存中处理该内容，并仅发送给弹窗中选中的 AI 助手。对应服务会按照各自的条款和隐私政策处理收到的内容。扩展不会截取或保存 AI 助手的回复。

扩展仅申请以下 Chrome 权限：

- `tabs`：查找已打开的受支持网站标签页，将其导航到新对话，或创建后台标签页。
- `notifications`：发送失败时，通过本地系统通知列出失败的 AI 助手。

网站访问范围通过扩展清单限制在六个受支持的 AI 服务。扩展不使用远程代码、广告、跟踪像素或第三方分析工具。

如有问题，可以通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/algorirhy/one-ai-shortcut/issues) 提交。
